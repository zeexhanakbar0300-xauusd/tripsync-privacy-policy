TripSync Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

1. Introduction

TripSync is a travel cost management application that helps users plan trips, estimate fuel costs, manage travel budgets, and organize travel expenses.

2. Information and app permissions

Depending on the features you use, TripSync may process information you enter, such as trip names, destinations, distances, budgets, fuel costs, and other expense details. If you allow location access or use location search, the app may process your device location or the locations you search for.

3. How information is used

Information is used to provide trip and expense calculations, location search, route planning, weather information where available, and to save app preferences.

4. Third-party services

TripSync may use third-party services to provide maps, location search, routing, and weather features. These may include OpenStreetMap, Photon, OSRM, and Open-Meteo. When you use these features, relevant search terms, location information, or coordinates may be sent to the applicable service provider. Those providers process information under their own privacy policies.

5. Storage and retention

Trip and expense information and app preferences may be stored on your device. The exact information retained depends on the features and version of the app you use. You can remove locally stored app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app, subject to your device's behavior.

6. Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be transmitted to the third-party service providers described above when needed to operate the features you request.

7. Advertising

Advertising practices may change in future versions. If advertising or analytics services are added, this policy will be updated to identify the relevant services and explain their data practices.

8. Children's privacy

TripSync is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Your choices

You can manage location permissions through your device settings and choose whether to use location-based features. You can stop using the app at any time.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page.
